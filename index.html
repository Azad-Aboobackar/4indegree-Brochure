University Locations

Life Around Georgian Campuses

All nine partner universities sit inside Tbilisi, the capital of Georgia — eight offering English-taught MBBS and one offering BSc Nursing. Shota Rustaveli Tbilisi International Airport is the single gateway, placing every campus within a 20–40 minute drive. One city, nine campuses, no domestic travel needed.

Nine Campuses, One City

Eight universities for English-taught MBBS and one for BSc Nursing — all located in Tbilisi. Campuses sit 2–7 km apart, so students share the same hostels, hospitals, food outlets and community wherever they enrol.

Travel & Connectivity

Tbilisi International Airport (TBS) is the single arrival point, 17 km from the city centre and 14–28 km from every campus. Direct and one-stop flights connect Delhi, Mumbai, Kochi and Dubai year-round.

Student Life

Affordable living ($250–400/month), a large Indian and Kerala community, Indian and halal messes near every campus, metro and minibus transport, and one of the safest capitals in the region.
